No account

Nothing to breach and no access to “recover”. You open the app and write.

No server

No analytics

What is inside

One app for several daily routines. You choose which of them to see.

Privacy as plumbing, not as a promise

Every statement below has a counterpart in the app's code. The full list is in the privacy policy.

  • The database is encrypted on disk.The key is generated on your device and held in the system keystore. The developer does not have it and cannot obtain it.
  • A lock on the way in.PIN, Face ID or fingerprint. The PIN is stored as a hash, not as a number.
  • Backups are encrypted with a passphrase only you know.The app never stores it. Where the file goes is your choice, in the system share sheet.
  • Export to JSON, CSV and Markdown.Your entries are yours, and they leave in one tap, in open formats anything can read.
  • Delete everything with one button.In settings, or simply by deleting the app: its storage goes with it. No need to ask the developer, who has none of your data.
  • No account, server, analytics, ads or trackers.No cross-app tracking either. There is nothing to track.

The one thing that is downloaded

A language model for the AI section, about 123 MB, and only when you start it yourself in settings. It runs on the phone too: entry text never goes to the network.

Leave it off and the app works in full without it.
